HackerTube tracks which videos Hacker News commenters have recommended over nearly 20 years. YouTube tracks how many people watched them. Each answers a different question, and neither can see the other's answer. NerdLore puts the two side by side, so you can find the talks engineers swear by that most people have never seen.
